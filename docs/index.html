FAQ

How accurate is it?

It is a first-pass estimate: dischargeable energy is inferred from monthly peak and semi-peak use. A final design needs 15-minute demand data, your contract-capacity structure and supplier quotes. The math is checked line-for-line against the Python model in the repo.

Why is ancillary-service revenue excluded?

Taipower's dReg / E-dReg market is saturated, and clearing prices were near NT$0 by late 2025. Leaving it out keeps the numbers conservative.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The calculator is one static page that runs in your browser. You can also download it and use it offline.

What are the subsidy conditions?

MOEA industrial storage subsidy: NT$5 M per MWh, up to 50 %, 1–10 MWh, factories in industrial / science / tech parks, domestic cells, non-PRC communication modules and a 3-year evening peak-shift commitment at 80 %. Check the latest MOEA notice.
